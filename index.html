About Me

I am a Senior Project Associate in the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Bombay. My research focuses on Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Image Processing, with a particular emphasis on Image Super-Resolution, Image Restoration, and Contactless Biometric Recognition.

With a foundation in Signal Processing from my undergraduate studies, I transitioned into advanced areas of visual computing to explore how machines can perceive, reconstruct, and enhance visual data. My work lies at the intersection of deep neural networks and image processing, where I develop computational models capable of recovering and interpreting complex visual information.

I aim to design intelligent systems that address challenging real-world problems, including high-quality image reconstruction, restoration of degraded images, and robust contactless biometric recognition.

Current Role
