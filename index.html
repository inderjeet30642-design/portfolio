About

Research
profile

I am a Post-Doctoral Fellow in the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Bombay, working under the supervision of Prof. Vikram M. Gadre. I also contribute to the ANRF-PAIR IITB Hub & Spoke initiative, spearheading collaborative research, technical seminars, workshops, and outreach programs.

My primary research interests span digital signal and image processing, deep learning, image restoration and enhancement, and contactless biometrics. With a foundation in signal processing from my undergraduate and postgraduate studies, I moved into visual computing to explore how machines can perceive, reconstruct, and enhance visual data.

I design intelligent systems for real-world problems — high-quality image reconstruction, restoration of degraded images, and contactless biometric recognition — sitting at the intersection of deep neural networks and classical image and signal processing.

PhD Thesis · 2021–2026

Deep Learning-based Approaches for Single-Image Super-Resolution

Developed a family of SISR frameworks — CMOA-Net, RMRDN, MDTN, and an edge-aware detail-preserving network among others — using hierarchical feature utilization, edge-guided reconstruction, and lightweight architectures. Explored CNNs, Vision Transformers, GANs, VAEs, DDPMs, and State-Space Models to improve detail recovery and computational efficiency. Guided student projects in dehazing, underwater restoration, inpainting, and low-light enhancement that grew out of this work.

M.Tech Thesis · 2019–2020

Design of a Lower-Order IIR Filter from a Higher-Order FIR

Built an automated filter-approximation framework using Particle Swarm Optimization to derive IIR transfer functions via direct coefficient estimation and root-polynomial formulation, with a bisection method to dynamically determine minimal filter order. Evaluated MSE, H2-norm, H∞-norm and L1-norm cost functions, establishing MSE optimization as the ideal baseline for phase linearity and system stability.
