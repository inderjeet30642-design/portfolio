About

Research
profile

I am a Post-Doctoral Fellow in the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Bombay, working under the supervision of Prof. Vikram M. Gadre. I also contribute to the ANRF-PAIR IITB Hub & Spokes initiative, facilitating high-impact collaborative research and outreach.

My primary research interests span digital signal and image processing, deep learning, image restoration and enhancement, and biometrics. With a foundation in signal processing from my undergraduate studies, I moved into visual computing to explore how machines can perceive, reconstruct, and enhance visual data.

I design intelligent systems for real-world problems — high-quality image reconstruction, restoration of degraded images, and contactless biometric recognition — sitting at the intersection of deep neural networks and classical image processing.
