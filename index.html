About Me

I am a Post-Doctoral Fellow in the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Bombay, working under the supervision of Prof. Vikram M. Gadre. I also contribute to the ANRF-PAIR IITB Hub & Spokes initiative, facilitating high-impact collaborative research and outreach. My primary research interests include Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Image Restoration/Enhancement, and Biometrics.

With a foundation in Signal Processing from my undergraduate studies, I transitioned into advanced areas of visual computing to explore how machines can perceive, reconstruct, and enhance visual data. My work lies at the intersection of deep neural networks and image processing, where I develop computational models capable of recovering and interpreting complex visual information.

I aim to design intelligent systems that address challenging real-world problems, including high-quality image reconstruction, restoration of degraded images, and contactless biometric recognition in collaboration with national stakeholders such as UIDAI, SAMEER, and SITAA.
